# Fun & pop-culture question brief

Read data/BRIEF.md first for the file format. These new questions should feel FUN, casual and broadly relatable, not academic.
Good examples: "Name something that uses suction.", "Name a Minecraft block.", "Name a technology brand.", "Name something you'd find in a junk drawer.", "Name a TikTok trend.", "Name a famous YouTuber.", "Name a candy that comes in a wrapper."

Hard rules (the validator enforces the first two):
1. Every question has at least one answer in EACH tier 10, 15, 30, 60, 85, and EXACTLY ONE answer at 100 (the single answer people are least likely to say).
2. Minimum 6 answers; aim for 60-150 answers for broad/creative prompts. Brainstorm exhaustively: include every reasonable answer a player could type, including creative lateral-thinking ones that a fair judge would accept.
3. Rarity = how likely an average American adult is to SAY that answer, NOT how big/important the thing is. First things people blurt out = 10/15.
4. American spelling for questions and canonical answers; British spellings as aliases. Add aliases for nicknames, abbreviations, brand vs. product names, common alternate phrasings.
5. Avoid crude/sexual answers. Avoid time-sensitive facts unless pinned ("before 2025"). Only include answers you are confident are correct.
6. Do NOT duplicate any question already in the database. The full list of existing question texts is in the scratchpad file named in your task.
7. Question text ends with a period, under ~70 characters. Vary phrasing.

Validate: `node tools/validate.js <your files>` must end with OK.


## UPDATE: the 'Too Clever' (15) tier is retired. Use only 10, 30, 60, 85, 100. Do not write new 15s.
